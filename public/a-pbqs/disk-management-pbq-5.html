CompTIA A+ PBQ Task

Your Task:

You have just installed a new 1.0 TB hard drive (Disk 1). You must prepare it for use.

  • Open the Disk Management utility.
  • Create a new simple volume on Disk 1 using all available space.
  • Assign the drive letter E:.
  • Format the volume with the NTFS file system.
  • Set the volume label to Data.
  • Perform a quick format.

Click "Done" on the desktop when you have completed the task.
